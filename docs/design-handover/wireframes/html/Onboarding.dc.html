Before you start

Brahma walks a curated clinical knowledge graph with you. Every line it shows is tied to a graph node and its source. If the graph has no grounded answer, Brahma says so and does not guess.

Decision support, not diagnosis. You stay responsible for every clinical decision, prescription and order. Brahma's suggestions are prompts to check, not instructions.
Your acceptance is recorded once, with the date and knowledge version, in your audit log.

1 · Talk to Brahma

Type the case the way you'd tell a colleague. Brahma reads back what it understood. When it needs something specific, it asks with a form inside the chat.
