### Summary

The security-requirement mapping is mostly concrete and consistent with C1 and the functional design, and the hardening is proportionate for a local app. It is not ready for four reasons. Claim matching adds a rounding tolerance and float equality that contradict "exact". Immutability and the single-live-version rule are only partly enforced. Four derived NFRs have no design. The shared database helper U1 relies on is defined nowhere.
