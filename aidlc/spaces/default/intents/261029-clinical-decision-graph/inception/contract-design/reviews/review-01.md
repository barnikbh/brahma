### Summary

Every DAG edge is covered by a contract, and the streaming contract honours the check-before-display rule. Three integration gaps would force rework: C5 and the other provider interfaces lack operations that the C8 routes and consumers need, the walk and map contract leaves resume, explore and failure behaviour undefined, and the plain-mode benchmark has no way to be scored through GroundingGuard. All three are small additions to this document.
