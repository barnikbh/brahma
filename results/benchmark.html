Methodology

Each case runs twice on the same model: once as a full Brahma walk (graph arm), once as a single answer with only the pack's source titles and no graph content (plain arm). Graph grounded share is emitted lines over emitted plus held-back lines from the walk's own counts. Plain grounded share is recommendation lines whose cited pack source exists and whose every claim is supported by a node under a cited source, using the same claim check as the walk guard. Agreement is backing nodes (cited for graph, supporting for plain) hitting the expected pathway over expected nodes. Expected pathways are derived deterministically from the pack's traversal over each case's findings and answers — never hand-written. Uro cases are shown but not counted toward the verdict.
